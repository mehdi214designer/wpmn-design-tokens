The story behind the product

We started building WordPress plugins in 2016 with a simple belief: business tools should not feel like business tools. They should feel fast, friendly, and obvious. The first release was a small form plugin built for our own agency projects.

That plugin grew into a family of products covering forms, CRM, support, scheduling, and commerce. Today they run on more than 300,000 websites, from one-person blogs to enterprise networks, all maintained by a team that still answers its own support tickets.

Every product follows the same playbook. Performance first, no artificial limits on free versions, and an interface a non-technical person can learn in an afternoon. We ship weekly, document everything, and treat each refund request as a design lesson.

The roadmap is public, votes are counted, and the most requested feature usually lands within a quarter. That loop between users and builders is the entire company strategy, and it has not changed since day one.
