FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is FluentForms really free?

Yes. The free version is genuinely free — no trial period, no credit card required. It includes unlimited forms, core fields, and email notifications. Upgrade to Pro when you need advanced features.

How is FluentForms different from Gravity Forms or WPForms?

FluentForms is significantly faster, more affordable, and includes features like conditional logic and analytics that competitors charge extra for. We don't charge per-site licenses on the free tier.

Can I use FluentForms with any WordPress theme?

Yes. FluentForms works with any well-coded WordPress theme. It integrates with Elementor, Gutenberg, Divi, Beaver Builder, and all major page builders.

Is my form data secure?

All submissions are stored in your WordPress database. We don't access your data. FluentForms includes built-in spam protection, hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, and GDPR compliance tools.

Can I migrate from another form plugin?

Yes. We offer migration tools for Gravity Forms, WPForms, and Contact Form 7. Import your forms and keep your existing submission data.
